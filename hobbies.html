Continuous Learning

I believe that learning extends far beyond the classroom. I enjoy exploring new technologies and continuously building my knowledge of information technology and cybersecurity through independent study and hands-on experiences.

  • Attending technology and cybersecurity seminars and workshops.
  • Watching educational YouTube channels and online technical tutorials to expand my programming and cybersecurity knowledge.
  • Researching emerging technologies, cybersecurity trends, and best practices.
  • Practicing problem-solving by exploring real-world technology scenarios and troubleshooting techniques.

Hands-On Hardware Experience

One of my favorite ways to learn is by taking technology apart to understand how it works. I enjoy examining computer hardware and peripheral devices, identifying components, and learning how systems are assembled.

  • Disassembled and examined an older Lenovo laptop to better understand internal computer components.
  • Disassembled an HP printer to learn about printer hardware, mechanical systems, and electronic components.
  • Developed a stronger understanding of hardware architecture through observation and research.

Community Service

Giving back to my community is an important part of who I am. Through volunteering, I have had opportunities to support students and families while strengthening my leadership and communication skills.

  • After-School Tutor – Assisted students with homework and academic enrichment at The Lincoln Center in Tallahassee.

Personal Interests

Outside of academics and technology, I enjoy activities that encourage creativity and balance. Crocheting allows me to develop patience and attention to detail, while dancing and exploring new music provide opportunities to relax and express creativity.

Looking Ahead

I am committed to lifelong learning and hope to continue expanding my knowledge in cybersecurity, cloud computing, networking, and digital forensics. My goal is to combine technical expertise, leadership, and community service to make meaningful contributions within the technology industry.
